# Recommendations

The SQL side of recommendations, the deck corpus and play rates, and combos. Back to [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md).

### Recommendations (SQL side)

- **A request scores nothing in SQL.** It reads the precompute worker's tables through the serving functions (above) and ranks in TypeScript. Nothing in the app calls the per-request functions `rec_add_candidates`, `rec_swap_candidates` and `rec_card_roles` or the timeout retry (closing T008); they leave the database with `rec_timeouts` once the release is live (T067). `rec_functional_tag_count` stays for a swap's tag count.
- **The serving pools filter through the covering index `cards_rec_pool`** (`id` include `color_identity`, `game_changer`, `name`, `mana_value`, `equivalence_base_id`, where the row is live, commander-legal and not a basic land), so a request never sweeps the whole `cards` heap. Keep the index's columns and predicate in step with what the pool functions read and filter on; the price update never writes these columns, so `cards` keeps its HOT updates.
- **The swap similarity** (`precompute_substitutes`, run by the worker): idf-weighted functional tag similarity through the tag closure, blended half and half with shared role roots, functional twins first. Exclusive tag modes (`app_config.functional_tag_exclusive_groups`, currently sweeper vs spot removal) multiply it by `penalty` when a candidate has only a mode the target lacks. It runs with `enable_nestloop = off` (repeat the SET on any `create or replace`): its argument-dependent CTEs are estimated at about one row.
- The swap pool's order (`precompute_substitutes`, `serving_swap_candidates`) reads `app_config.scoring`'s no-collection swap weights for tag similarity, staple score and mana value, and its mana value falloff; the partner pool (`serving_partner_pool`) reads its corpus score shape. The substitutes worker hashes those weights, so moving them rebuilds every list.
- The add pool's order is `commander_card_scores.pool_score`, the corpus score over the commander's sources at their weights (√baseline without commander decks), which the retired `rec_add_candidates` computed per request. The app re-scores with `app_config.scoring.weights.add` (corpus 0.7, role gap 0.2, deck affinity 0.1 since T064) and groups by `cardCategory`.
- Cuts use commander play rates only at or above `minDecks`: `LOW_SYNERGY` below 0.35, and cards scoring ≥ 0.5 aren't flagged for cost or role overlap (generic role targets undercount what some commanders run).
- Role checks use `commander_stats.role_profile`: the average cards per deck in each `deck_role_targets` role, from `loadRoleCards`, the same rule as `card_roles`. `roleTargetsFor` (`@mtg/core/scoring` `rank.ts`) moves the generic targets toward that profile by `commanderShare`, for both cut redundancy and add role gaps.
- **The learned skeleton (T062, migration `20261006000800`):** `commander_stats` also holds `curve_profile` (nonland cards per deck at each mana value, `'0'` to `'7'`, the last for 7 and up), `land_count` and `basic_land_count`, written with the rest of a commander's stats (`lib/key-stats.ts`, diff-only; `corpus.decks.basic_lands` counts the basics). `commander_sets` holds EDHREC's role and curve profiles for the commander's page: the summed inclusion of its listed cards per role and per mana value, scaled so the curve totals `app_config.scoring.skeleton.typicalNonlandCards` (64: pages are trimmed and sum to about 45), read per request through `serving_commander_profile`. The `curve` score component (the deck's shortfall at a card's mana value against the commander's curve, `@mtg/core/scoring` `curve.ts`) is computed for adds at weight 0; `skeleton.curveCuts` (HIGH_MANA_VALUE from the curve instead of `cuts.highManaValue`) and `skeleton.edhrecPrior` (role targets and the curve moved toward EDHREC's profile first) are off. All three failed or tied the evaluation (2026-10-06, time split; `docs/tasks.md` T062); builds (T063) read the curve and land counts directly.

- **Builds (T063, contract v24, migration `20261006001100`):** `buildDeck` (`@mtg/core/scoring` `build.ts`, tested) builds a 99 for a commander and a bracket (scoring-design.md, "Mode C"). Lands and basic lands aim for the commander's `land_count` and `basic_land_count` as its decks earn a share (`commanderShare`), a typical count for its colour count (`app_config.scoring.build.landCounts`, `basicLandCounts`) for the rest. Nonland slots fill greedily by the add score against the deck so far (`weights.add`, the one marginal-value function: role, curve and affinity move with each pick; `affinityTracker` keeps affinity incremental), ties by name; then nonbasic lands the same way; then basics split by the coloured mana symbols of the spells and commanders (pure share, largest remainder; even without symbols; Wastes for colourless). A build treats every bracket rule as a limit: Game Changers, mass land denial, extra-turn cards past the limit, and a card that would complete a combo above the bracket are skipped. From a collection it picks owned cards only (conflicts never count) and stops once the best card left scores under `build.qualityFloor` (0.35): what stays open is the feasibility report, and `fill: 'value'` fills the nonland slots left with unowned priced cards by (score − floor) per dollar (`collection.priceFloorUsd`). Without a collection it fills every slot. Kept cards (the context's main cards) are scored against the rest of the build. `serving_build_pool` reads everything in one round: the add pool (the collection's and, beside it, everyone's), the pairs with both cards among them and every card's weight, `serving_deck_combos` over them (each missing card's row once) and the basic lands by name.

### Deck corpus and play rates

- **Where decks live:** the daily crawl (T036) writes raw decks into `archidekt.decks`; players save theirs in `public.decks`. `cli collate` puts every deck that passes the corpus rule into `corpus.decks`, and the precompute worker's per-commander pass and nightly baseline count only that (as does `aggregate:corpus`, the full rebuild). Only aggregates are public tables.
- **The corpus rule** (`checkCorpusDeck`, `@mtg/core/commander`): a legal commander or a legal pair, every card known to the catalog and inside the commanders' colours, exactly 100 cards. The collator applies it to every deck source, players' decks included, public or private. Card legality beyond the commanders is not part of it: a deck last edited before a ban still says what players run with that commander, and recommendations leave banned cards out on their own (3.7% of crawled decks held a banned or not-legal card on 2026-10-05). It drops decks whose two "commanders" aren't a legal pair (`isValidPartnerPair`): Archidekt's Commander category also holds companions and misfiled cards.
  - `aggregate:corpus` checks the commanders again (`resolveDeck`, `apps/worker/src/lib/corpus.ts`, through the same `corpusCommanders`) and tolerates `maxUnresolvedCards` cards that have left the catalog since collation. It counts a deck posted on two sites once (same `content_hash`, another source); identical decks on one site are different players' and each count.
- **Stored stats:** `card_global_stats.rate` is the baseline p0, computed over decks whose identity allows the card. `commander_card_stats` holds inclusion shrunk toward p0, (x + α·p0)/(n + α), and synergy = shrunk − p0.
- **Settings:** `app_config.corpus` holds `shrinkAlpha`, `minDecks` and `fullDecks` (both 50 since 2026-10-05, owner decision; `fullDecks` was 100), `partnerPoolWeight` (0.25), `edhrecPriorCap` (100), `severeSynergyScore` (0.2), the aggregate's `maxUnresolvedCards` and the collator's `collateMaxRemovedShare` (0.1).
- **Every scoring change goes through the offline evaluation** (T058, `cli eval:holdout`, settings in `app_config.scoring.eval`): it holds out a seeded 10% of the collated decks, rebuilds the play rates from the rest in memory and grades the app's own ranking (`@mtg/core/scoring` `rank.ts`) on them. The gate (`evaluateGate`, `@mtg/core/scoring` `evaluate.ts`): overall adds recall@20 rises with a 95% bootstrap interval above zero (resampled over commanders), no size bucket falls by more than its own interval's half-width, the Sol Ring rate rises by no more than `solRingTolerance`, and the regression fixtures pass (run separately; the report says so). It gives no pass on fewer than `eval.minCommanders` (200) held-out commanders. Anything the EDHREC prior touches is gated on the time split, `--time-split --snapshot-month` pinned to the month of the EDHREC fetch the run is compared against: the live month moves with every fetch. A new scoring component ships at weight 0 until it passes.
- **Scoring weights and thresholds** (T057, migration `20261006000300`): `app_config.scoring`, private, holds the add and swap weights, the corpus score's shape (`synergyScale`, `synergyShare`, `baselineWeight`, `neutralValue`), the swap thresholds and every cut threshold. `@mtg/core/scoring` validates it and `app_config.corpus` (`parseScoringConfig`, `parseCorpusSettings`): a missing or malformed value is an error, never a fallback, and no scoring number stays in code. The ranking itself is `@mtg/core/scoring` `rank.ts` (`rankAdds`, `rankCuts`, `rankSwaps`), pure functions over loaded rows that the app, the regression fixtures and the evaluation share. The worker re-scores everything when `app_config.scoring.corpus` moves (`precompute_state`).
- **Release-aware counting:** a card counts only against decks updated in or after its release month.
  - Release month comes from `card_stats.first_printed_at`, never `cards.released_at`: Oracle Cards dates a card by its representative, often latest, printing, which made Sol Ring look new.
  - Per-card eligible counts live in `card_global_stats.eligible_decks` and `commander_card_stats.eligible_decks`. `commander_stats.deck_months` and `corpus_identity_stats` hold decks per month (and per identity) for cards no deck runs.
  - `corpusComponent` returns null (unknown, not low) when too few decks anywhere could have run a card. Add skips those cards.
- **Partner pooling (web side):** `apps/web/src/lib/server/corpus.ts` loads every commander key that shares one of a deck's commanders; `pickCorpusSources` (`@mtg/core/scoring`) decides which count.
  - A key with `minDecks` decks of its own stands alone. Below that, every other key led by one of its commanders is borrowed at `partnerPoolWeight` per deck. A card's eligible decks count only sources whose colours allow it, and confidence never reaches `full` while borrowing.
  - `CommanderKeyRef.deckCount` counts only decks with exactly those commanders; `borrowedDeckCount` holds the rest, and `CorpusEvidence.pooled` marks weighted counts. Commander pages need decks of their own.
  - Why 0.25: borrowed decks predicted a pair's own top cards better than colour-only rates, but every pair measured includes Rograkh; retune when more pair data exists (T021).
- **Scoring:** `@mtg/core/scoring` `corpusComponent` blends commander and baseline scores. The baseline alone gets half the corpus weight, and commander decks ramp it to full weight at `fullDecks`.
- **Commander deck lookups** (contract v2): a deck whose commander has no corpus can request decks.
  - Queue: `public.commander_requests`, one active row per commander (partial unique index). API roles only reach it through `get_commander_request`, `request_commander_decks` (rate limit per salted visitor hash, queue cap, cooldowns) and `get_commander_request_status`. Limits and the ETA pace live in `app_config.commander_requests`.
  - **Served through the crawl by the VPS worker** (`apps/worker/src/jobs/lookups.ts`, T066; the PC worker that wrote into the deck spike's file was retired 2026-10-05). `crawl_next_commanders` hands out a commander with an active lookup first, adding it to `crawl.queue` if the seed list doesn't know it. The worker moves a request to `collecting`, starts a crawl run if none is going (at most every `crawlTriggerMinutes`), and once the queue row shows a visit after the request started: `collate` and the precompute worker's per-commander pass, then `done` with the commander's corpus decks, or `not_enough_decks` below `minDecks` or when the crawl found none (`not_found`, `no_led_decks`). A switched-off source fails it with the reason; no visit within `visitTimeoutMinutes` fails it too. A commander already `not_found` (never handed out again) ends `not_enough_decks` at once, and a visit earlier in the run going now counts, since that run won't hand the commander out twice. One Archidekt client, so lookups keep the crawl's pace, claim and kill switch.
  - The deck tool shows the collector offline while the worker's `worker_status` heartbeat (`commander-requests`, every 10 s) is over 30 s old: until the worker is deployed, requests wait.
  - When a status action first sees `done`, it calls `updateTag("corpus")` and `updateTag("recs")`.

### Combos (Commander Spellbook)

`sync:spellbook` loads Commander Spellbook's combo database into the raw `spellbook` schema: card-to-card relationships that hold whatever the commander, where the corpus only says what decks happen to run. Loaded locally (2026-10-05: 113,025 combos, 1,099 results, 42 MB, none malformed). The collator resolves them into `corpus.spellbook_combos` (111,498 locally: banned combos are left out, and so is any combo naming a card the catalog lacks or a tag or result status it doesn't know). The precompute worker builds `spellbook_combo_pieces` and `spellbook_combo_details` from it, which the bracket rules and the "complete a combo" Add group read (T060, below).

- **One request a day, to the published export.** `json.commanderspellbook.com/variants.json.gz` (about 29 MB gzipped, rebuilt daily) is an S3 bucket behind CloudFront, served with `content-encoding: gzip`, so `fetch` hands back the JSON unpacked. The run skips when its `Last-Modified` is unchanged since the last success. Never call `backend.commanderspellbook.com`: its robots.txt disallows every path.
- **The export is one 680 MB line**, past V8's string limit, so neither `JSON.parse` nor `readJsonl` can read it. `JsonRootStream` (`@mtg/core/parse`) splits the `variants` array into elements as bytes arrive; `spellbookVariant` reduces each to what raw keeps.
- **Raw, as published.** One `spellbook.combos` row per Spellbook variant (`variant_id`, one exact set of cards): the pieces as Scryfall oracle ids (`card_oracle_ids uuid[]`, ascending, names aligned in `card_names`), the pieces that must be the commander, template pieces by name, `spellbook.features` ids, the bracket tag and the mana needed. Nothing is resolved against our catalog, so the sync needs nothing loaded first. A tag or result status Spellbook adds later is stored as published for the collator to interpret; only a combo missing an id, an oracle id or a name is refused. The sanity gate refuses more than 1% malformed or fewer than 90% of the previous run's combos.
- **Results** are `spellbook.features`: status `S` (standalone, "Win the game") and `C` (contextual) are results; `H` are hidden steps Spellbook chains combos through and never shows. **Bracket tags** are Spellbook's own (`SPELLBOOK_BRACKET_TAGS`): R Ruthless (bracket 4), S Spicy and P Powerful (3), O Oddball and C Core (2), E Exhibition (1), B banned in Commander.
- Not stored: step descriptions and prerequisites (about 50 MB of prose a day; link to `commanderspellbook.com/combo/<variant_id>/` instead), prices, images, other formats, salt. `edhrec_deck_count` is Spellbook's count of EDHREC decks (its `popularity`), so like EDHREC's numbers it is never displayed, and it never leaves raw.
- **Not exposed:** the schema is `service_role` only, RLS on, no API grants. Whatever shows combos credits and links Commander Spellbook wherever they appear (every `ComboRef` carries its `url`).
- **Bracket rules (T060, contract v21, migration `20261006000700`):** `estimateBracket` (`@mtg/core/commander`, tested) is the lowest bracket in 2–4 the deck fits under Game Changers (`gameChangerLimit`, WotC's list, in code), mass land denial (none below `app_config.brackets.massLandDenialFromBracket`, 4), extra-turn cards (`maxExtraTurnCards`: 0 in bracket 1, 2 in brackets 2–3), an extra-turn loop (a complete combo with an `extraTurnLoopResults` result, none below 4) and the highest minimum bracket among its complete combos (Spellbook's tag: E 1, O and C 2, S and P 3, R 4; a combo with a template piece isn't checked). Mass land denial and extra turns are Tagger's tags (`massLandDenialTagIds`, `extraTurnTagIds`), planeswalkers left out, read through `bracket_cards()` (cached). No tutor limit (WotC, October 2025). Mass land denial below its bracket is a hard rule: kept out of adds and swaps, and a mandatory cut (`OVER_BRACKET_MLD`); an over-bracket combo's pieces (`OVER_BRACKET_COMBO`) and extra turns past the limit or in a loop (`OVER_BRACKET_EXTRA_TURNS`) are flags, suggested cuts at the optional cut cap. A piece of a complete combo the bracket allows is never cut for its play rate alone.
  - **`serving_deck_combos(cards, commanders, exclude, allowGameChangers, near)`** returns the deck's complete combos and, with `near`, those one named card short, each missing card a `serving_card` that passes the deck's filters; a commander piece counts only as a commander. Deck analysis reads complete ones (`DeckAnalysis.combos`, `bracketSignals`), cuts too, and adds read both: **"complete a combo"** (`AddResult.combos`) lists the one-short combos the bracket allows, one per missing card, by what the combo does (`app_config.scoring.combos.resultClasses`: winning, then infinite mana, damage or turns, then other standalone results, then contextual ones) and then the missing card's add score; an add that would complete a combo above the bracket carries `completesOverBracket`. The estimator's agreement with authors' declared brackets is in `eval:holdout`'s report (hosted's crawl has stored 4,449 declared brackets since T056).
